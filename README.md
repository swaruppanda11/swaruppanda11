## About

I build cross-platform mobile and desktop software. Currently at ICE, I'm extending a production KMP app to macOS and Windows using Compose Multiplatform — with intentional architectural separation between mobile and desktop UI layers. Prior iOS internships at OneBanc Technologies and Infosys. Selected in the top 50 of 750 applicants for Apple's iOS Developer Training Program.

On the research side, I have 60+ Google Scholar citations, three published books on AI/ML and distributed systems, and IEEE conference papers spanning DevOps reliability, agentic AI, Kubernetes orchestration, and urban climate forecasting. I presented at IC3ET 2026 (Mumbai) and AIMLCPS 2026.
